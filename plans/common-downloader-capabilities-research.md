# Common desktop download-manager capabilities

Research date: 2026-08-13 (refreshes the original 2026-08-11 survey)
Scope: current, user-facing desktop capabilities documented by the products themselves. Sources are limited to official product sites, official help centers, official release notes, official repositories, and protocol/engine manuals. A blank or unmentioned capability below means “not established by the reviewed source,” not “the product definitely lacks it.” This is a market-capability benchmark, not an audit of Kite’s current implementation or a hands-on test of competitor binaries.

Evidence labels used in the 2026-08-13 refresh:

- **[Fact — released/current docs]**: an official product page, help page, or released-version note explicitly documents the behavior.
- **[Fact — engine primitive]**: the official engine/manual provides the primitive, but this does not prove that a desktop product exposes it in its UI.
- **[Fact — repository main]**: an official repository’s current default branch documents or contains the behavior; it is not counted as a released desktop capability unless a release source also confirms it.
- **[Inference]**: a product/design implication synthesized from multiple facts, not a claim made by a competitor.
- **[Not established]**: the reviewed official sources do not prove the behavior. This is deliberately not written as “unsupported.”

## Executive conclusion

The mature-product baseline is broader than protocol support. Across the reviewed products, the recurring expectations are:

1. **Downloads enter without friction:** browser interception/context menus, one-click page capture, clipboard/link collection, and “download all links.”
2. **Work survives disruption:** automatic resume, error recovery, persistent queues, retry policy, and safe behavior across app/computer restarts.
3. **Transfers obey policy:** queue priority, concurrency, time schedules, bandwidth schedules/quotas, per-task overrides, and completion actions.
4. **Protected and rich content works:** credentials/cookies, proxy or VPN binding, streaming-video discovery, quality/audio/subtitle selection, and media post-processing.
5. **Completion is a workflow:** archive extraction, antivirus or external-command hooks, notifications, Music/TV integration, and shutdown/sleep actions.
6. **The desktop app is not the only control surface:** authenticated Web UI/API, mobile/remote submission, RSS automation, and search.
7. **Distribution is part of reliability:** signed/notarized app delivery, an updater, release notes, architecture-specific builds, and extension distribution.

For a general macOS downloader, the strongest “table stakes” are items 1–3 plus authentication/proxy. Media, archive, remote/RSS, and automation differentiate a mature product from a protocol front end.

It covers Free Download Manager, Motrix/aria2, JDownloader 2, Folx, and Downie. The products have different scopes, so a specialized media downloader is not penalized for omitting BitTorrent, and an engine primitive is not presented as a finished UI.

### What “preview” means in mature products

The official evidence separates four concepts that should not be collapsed into one label:

1. **[Fact] Input review** — Motrix preserves a non-empty form and accepts rich request context; Folx lets the user adjust tags/schedule/destination; JDownloader stages discovered links before start. Sources: [Motrix 1.8.19](https://github.com/agalwood/Motrix/releases/tag/v1.8.19) · [Folx](https://mac.eltima.com/download-manager.html) · [JDownloader LinkGrabber](https://support.jdownloader.org/en/knowledgebase/article/linkgrabber-how-to-add-links).
2. **[Fact] Availability/file-tree inspection** — aria2 can dry-run direct downloads and enumerate torrent/Metalink files; JDownloader deep-crawls a submitted page; Downie resolves media renditions. Sources: [aria2](https://aria2.github.io/manual/en/html/aria2c.html) · [JDownloader](https://support.jdownloader.org/en/knowledgebase/article/linkgrabber-how-to-add-links) · [Downie](https://software.charliemonroe.net/help/downie/?article=quality).
3. **[Fact] Content preview** — FDM’s audio/video preview happens while a download is incomplete. It does not prove preflight metadata resolution. Source: [FDM](https://www.freedownloadmanager.org/features.htm).
4. **[Fact] Output preview** — Downie lets the user choose rendition and post-processing before transfer and can expose metadata, thumbnails, subtitles, and the eventual format. Sources: [Downie quality](https://software.charliemonroe.net/help/downie/?article=quality) · [settings](https://software.charliemonroe.net/help/downie/?article=preferences) · [post-processing](https://software.charliemonroe.net/help/downie/?article=postprocessing).

**[Inference]** A general macOS downloader should therefore present a progressive preflight rather than wait for one blocking “perfect preview” response:

- Parse immediately: identify direct URL, magnet, torrent/Metalink file, media page, cURL, deep link, or invalid input; show the number of accepted, duplicate, and rejected inputs.
- Probe without committing: show `Checking…`, allow cancellation/retry, preserve the original input, and never dismiss a non-empty form because of an outside click or Escape without confirmation.
- For HTTP/FTP, surface effective host/redirect, proposed filename, MIME/type, known or unknown size, resume/range availability, destination and free-space fit, file-conflict policy, selected credential/proxy profile, and checksum when supplied. Redact cookie/Authorization values.
- For torrent/Metalink, surface display name/info hash, total and selected size, a searchable file tree with selection and priority, tracker/private status when known, destination, and seeding policy.
- For media, surface title/site/thumbnail, playlist count, renditions (resolution, container, codecs, audio/subtitles, estimated size when the source exposes one), final post-processing format, and whether merging/conversion is required.
- Offer explicit outcomes: **Download now**, **Add paused**, **Add to queue**, or **Schedule**. Keep persistent defaults visible as summaries and expose per-task overrides progressively.
- Treat `unknown`, `login required`, `proxy failed`, `unsupported/DRM`, `permission denied`, and `insufficient disk space` as distinct states with an action, not one generic error.

The HTTP/FTP probe and torrent/Metalink file-tree parts above are implementable on top of aria2 primitives documented in its [option/RPC manual](https://aria2.github.io/manual/en/html/aria2c.html); that statement is about engine feasibility, not proof of any existing Kite UI.

## Product-specific observations

### Internet Download Manager

The official feature page is a useful benchmark for a classic file downloader: automatic browser takeover, dynamic segmentation, recovery after shutdown/power loss, multiple scheduled queues, site spider/grabber, categories, “download all,” quotas, antivirus invocation, proxy/authentication support, and weekly update checks. Its download flow is delivered as a Windows `.exe`, so it is a capability benchmark rather than a macOS implementation model. Source: [IDM official features](https://www.internetdownloadmanager.com/features2.html).

### Free Download Manager

FDM 6.34 is currently presented as cross-platform for Windows, macOS, Android, and Linux. Its current official features combine browser interception, segmented/resumable downloads, BitTorrent/magnets, add-on-based site/video support, audio/video preview and conversion, scheduler, traffic modes, categories, and Android-to-desktop remote submission. Source: [FDM home](https://www.freedownloadmanager.org/) and [features](https://www.freedownloadmanager.org/features.htm).

### Motrix and aria2

The shipping and development evidence must be separated. On 2026-08-13, the public Motrix site still points to v1.8.19, whose official tag documents HTTP/FTP/BitTorrent/magnet support, torrent file selection, tracker updates, UPnP/NAT-PMP, concurrency, speed limits, and the detailed New Task improvements listed above. The repository default branch now describes Motrix Turbo with a broader MDXP/browser-extension/headless/plugin/recovery architecture; those are repository-main facts, not independently verified v1.8.19 binary behavior. aria2 1.37 supplies lower-level dry-run, file listing/selection, authentication/proxy, checksum, queue/RPC, event-hook, and BitTorrent primitives, but an engine option is not proof of a polished Motrix setting or preview control. Sources: [public site](https://motrix.app/), [v1.8.19 release](https://github.com/agalwood/Motrix/releases/tag/v1.8.19), [v1.8.19 README](https://github.com/agalwood/Motrix/blob/v1.8.19/README.md), [repository main](https://github.com/agalwood/Motrix), and [aria2 manual](https://aria2.github.io/manual/en/html/aria2c.html).

### JDownloader 2

JDownloader’s distinctive scope is host/site integration rather than only transport protocols: its own browser extension and Click’n’Load flow, link collection/crawling, account and cookie login, browser-assisted captchas, proxy/VPN connection management, automatic archive extraction, headless/NAS operation through MyJDownloader, and extensibility through Packagizer/EventScripter. Current official downloads include macOS Tahoe and Apple-Silicon packages with published SHA-256 values. Sources: [link collection](https://support.jdownloader.org/en/knowledgebase/article/collect-and-download-links-from-unsupported-websites), [cookies](https://support.jdownloader.org/en/knowledgebase/article/account-cookie-login-instructions), [captchas](https://support.jdownloader.org/en/knowledgebase/article/jd-opens-my-browser-to-display-captchas), [archives](https://support.jdownloader.org/en/knowledgebase/article/what-shall-i-do-with-rar-files), [automation](https://support.jdownloader.org/en/knowledgebase/article/what-is-the-event-scripter), and [downloads](https://jdownloader.org/jdownloader2).

### qBittorrent

qBittorrent is the clearest benchmark for advanced torrent operation: integrated search, RSS/regex automation, DHT/PEX/LSD and encryption, Web UI, queueing and priority, file selection/priority, bandwidth scheduling, IP filtering, UPnP/NAT-PMP, VPN-interface binding, and completion scripts. Source: [qBittorrent official features](https://www.qbittorrent.org/), [VPN binding](https://github.com/qbittorrent/qBittorrent/wiki/How-to-bind-your-vpn-to-prevent-ip-leaks), and [external programs](https://github.com/qbittorrent/qBittorrent/wiki/External-programs-How-to).

### Folx and Downie (macOS-native commercial benchmarks)

Folx is the general-download/torrent benchmark: Safari and other browser integration, resume, proxy, Spotlight tags, scheduling, Smart Speed, credential storage, Music integration, multi-threading, magnet/torrent support, and torrent search. Its official page currently lists version 5.34.14042, released 2026-06-03. Source: [Folx official product page](https://mac.eltima.com/download-manager.html).

Downie shows how deep a focused media downloader goes: browser extensions, a built-in extraction browser, site/login handling, 4K and 1,000+ sites, HLS/DASH joining, quality and audio/subtitle choice, thumbnails/metadata, conversion or audio extraction, custom post-processing, iCloud history, proxy settings, search engines, and frequent integration updates. Source: [Downie product page](https://software.charliemonroe.net/downie/), [settings](https://software.charliemonroe.net/help/downie/?article=preferences), [extensions](https://software.charliemonroe.net/help/downie/?article=extensions), and [post-processing](https://software.charliemonroe.net/help/downie/?article=postprocessing).

## Suggested capability tiers for a macOS product

This ordering is an inference from the official product evidence above, not a claim made by any one source.

### Tier 1 — expected in a dependable general downloader

- Safari plus Chromium/Firefox capture packages, clipboard monitoring, and page/link batch capture.
- Durable resume/retry/crash recovery with actionable error states and file-conflict/disk-space policy.
- Queue order and priority, concurrency controls, schedules, global/per-task bandwidth limits, and sleep/shutdown/quit actions.
- Secure credentials/cookies, HTTP(S)/SOCKS proxy support, and optional no-direct-fallback/interface binding.
- Signed/notarized distribution, native update checks, release notes, integrity verification, and extension versioning.

### Tier 2 — mature workflow capability

- HLS/DASH/video discovery, quality/audio/subtitle selection, playlist and metadata handling, merge/conversion.
- Multipart archive verification, password workflow, auto-extraction, destination/cleanup rules.
- Safe completion hooks: notification, reveal/open, Music/TV import, checksum/scan command, and sandboxed scripts.
- Rule-based categorization by host, MIME/extension, size, source, destination, and schedule.

### Tier 3 — power-user and ecosystem capability

- Authenticated remote Web UI/API/mobile submission with a clearly separate threat model from localhost capture.
- RSS subscriptions with previewable filters, duplicate prevention, and automatic category/destination assignment.
- Torrent search providers, VPN leak prevention, IP filters, port mapping, seeding ratio/time automation.
- Maintained site integrations, browser-assisted extraction/captcha flows, headless operation, and extensibility without compromising the native app’s security boundary.
